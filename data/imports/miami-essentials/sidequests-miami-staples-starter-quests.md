# sidequests — Miami Staples Starter Quests

Seed quests that need no partner buy-in. Public places, nature, and Miami icons. Verification for these is photo capture plus GPS, since there's no venue QR. Rewards follow the Master Context tiers: Easy 50 XP / 25 Points, Medium 150 / 75, Hard 400 / 200, Legendary 1000+ / 500+.

Note: a few of these have an entry fee or cover (marked $). Nobody needs to sign anything, but the player pays to get in.

## Neighborhoods & Street Art

### 1. Wynwood Mural Chase

- **Where:** Wynwood, NW 2nd Ave corridor
- **Difficulty:** Medium · 150 XP · 75 Points · \~45 min
- **Description:** Wynwood is an open-air gallery that changes every year. Go find out what's on the walls right now.
- **Objective:** Photograph 10 different murals.
- **Completion:** 10 photo captures inside the Wynwood geofence.
- **Community Note prompt:** Which mural stopped you in your tracks?

### 2. Little Havana Domino Watch

- **Where:** Máximo Gómez (Domino) Park, Calle Ocho
- **Difficulty:** Easy · 50 XP · 25 Points · \~15 min
- **Description:** The clack of dominoes is the heartbeat of Calle Ocho.
- **Objective:** Watch a full game at Domino Park.
- **Completion:** Photo capture at the park.

### 3. Walk of Fame on Calle Ocho

- **Where:** Calle Ocho, SW 12th–17th Ave
- **Difficulty:** Medium · 150 XP · 75 Points · \~30 min
- **Description:** The stars on the sidewalk honor Latin music and culture legends.
- **Objective:** Find Celia Cruz's star and photograph it, plus 4 other stars.
- **Completion:** 5 photo captures on Calle Ocho.

### 4. Coconut Grove Banyan Hunt

- **Where:** Coconut Grove, Peacock Park and surrounding streets
- **Difficulty:** Easy · 50 XP · 25 Points · \~20 min
- **Description:** The Grove is Miami's oldest neighborhood, and its banyans are older than most of the city.
- **Objective:** Find a banyan tree big enough to stand inside its roots.
- **Completion:** Photo capture in Coconut Grove.

### 5. Art Deco Color Walk

- **Where:** Ocean Drive and Collins Ave, South Beach
- **Difficulty:** Medium · 150 XP · 75 Points · \~40 min
- **Description:** The world's biggest collection of Art Deco buildings, all in pastel.
- **Objective:** Photograph Deco buildings in pink, mint, yellow, and blue.
- **Completion:** 4 photo captures in the Art Deco Historic District.

## Beach & Water

### 6. Sunrise on South Pointe

- **Where:** South Pointe Park Pier, Miami Beach
- **Difficulty:** Easy · 50 XP · 25 Points · \~30 min
- **Description:** You haven't seen Miami until you've seen the sun come up over the Atlantic.
- **Objective:** Watch a sunrise from South Pointe Pier.
- **Completion:** Photo capture at the pier before 8 a.m.

### 7. Lifeguard Tower Collector

- **Where:** Miami Beach, 1st–20th St
- **Difficulty:** Medium · 150 XP · 75 Points · \~60 min
- **Description:** Every lifeguard tower on Miami Beach has its own design.
- **Objective:** Photograph 5 different lifeguard towers.
- **Completion:** 5 photo captures along the beach.

### 8. Key Biscayne Lighthouse ($)

- **Where:** Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park
- **Difficulty:** Medium · 150 XP · 75 Points · \~2 hrs
- **Description:** The oldest standing structure in Miami-Dade, at the tip of the key.
- **Objective:** Reach the Cape Florida Lighthouse.
- **Completion:** Photo capture at the lighthouse. Park entry fee applies.

### 9. Haulover Sandbar Day

- **Where:** Haulover Sandbar (boat, kayak, or paddleboard)
- **Difficulty:** Hard · 400 XP · 200 Points · \~half day
- **Description:** Miami's floating party in the middle of the bay.
- **Objective:** Get to the sandbar and stand in the water.
- **Completion:** Photo capture at the sandbar.

## Nature

### 10. Oleta River Paddle

- **Where:** Oleta River State Park ($)
- **Difficulty:** Hard · 400 XP · 200 Points · \~2 hrs
- **Description:** Florida's largest urban park, hiding mangrove tunnels in North Miami.
- **Objective:** Paddle through a mangrove trail.
- **Completion:** Photo capture on the water inside the park.

### 11. Everglades Gator Spotting

- **Where:** Shark Valley, Everglades National Park ($)
- **Difficulty:** Hard · 400 XP · 200 Points · \~3 hrs
- **Description:** The River of Grass is an hour from downtown.
- **Objective:** Spot an alligator (from a safe distance) and reach the Shark Valley observation tower.
- **Completion:** 2 photo captures. Keep 15+ feet from wildlife.

### 12. Matheson Hammock Atoll Pool

- **Where:** Matheson Hammock Park, Coral Gables ($ parking)
- **Difficulty:** Easy · 50 XP · 25 Points · \~1 hr
- **Description:** A man-made atoll pool fed by the bay, with skyline views.
- **Objective:** Swim or wade in the atoll pool.
- **Completion:** Photo capture at the pool.

### 13. Venetian Causeway Crossing

- **Where:** Venetian Causeway, Miami to Miami Beach
- **Difficulty:** Hard · 400 XP · 200 Points · \~1.5 hrs walking
- **Description:** Island-hop across Biscayne Bay on foot or by bike.
- **Objective:** Cross the full causeway end to end.
- **Completion:** Photo captures at both ends (GPS-verified).

## Nightlife & Culture

- **Where:** Club Space, Downtown
- **Difficulty:** Legendary · 1000 XP · 500 Points
- **Description:** Miami's most legendary dance floor goes until the sun comes up.
- **Objective:** Be on the terrace when the sunrise hits.
- **Completion:** Photo capture at Space after sunrise. 21+ only.

### 20. Ball & Chain Night ($ cover some nights)

- **Where:** Calle Ocho
- **Difficulty:** Medium · 150 XP · 75 Points
- **Description:** A legendary jazz and salsa spot since 1935.
- **Objective:** Dance one song to live music.
- **Completion:** Photo or video capture. 21+ evenings.

## Quest Line Idea

**The Miami Starter Pack:** Complete 5 of these to unlock a Legendary badge. It works as an onboarding quest line for new players.

## Before Publishing

- These need photo + GPS verification, not QR. That doesn't match the current QR-first MVP flow, so either decide on a no-QR quest type or put up QR stickers only where it's allowed.
- Check hours, fees, and access before going live.
- Any named venue (Space, Ball & Chain) could turn into a partner pitch later.

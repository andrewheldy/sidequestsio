# Illustration language

SideQuests illustrations are quiet editorial diagrams, not game art. They use one bold monoline weight, large areas of warm negative space, and at most one functional accent plus Reward Gold. Doorways and paths may recur as a narrative device, but not as a decorative stamp on every screen.

## Construction

- Canvas: 640 × 480, 48 px outer corner radius when presented on a tile.
- Primary line: 15 px at source, round caps and joins.
- Secondary line: 8 px.
- Base: Warm Sand 50; line: Midnight 900.
- Accent: Ocean for discovery, Palm for success, Coral for interruption, Gold for earned moments.
- People are simplified and inclusive; do not encode gender through clichés.
- Never use pseudo-3D coins, fantasy treasure chests, controllers, shields, or neon glows.

Each illustration includes an accessible SVG title and can be recolored by replacing the palette primitives. Loading motion may animate the dashed path only.

# SideQuests — Master Context

## Vision

SideQuests transforms real-world exploration into a game.

People discover places through interactive quests instead of traditional search, earning progression and rewards while creating memorable real-world experiences.

For users, SideQuests makes discovering a city fun.

For businesses, SideQuests turns customer visits into measurable engagement.

---

# Mission

Build the world's most engaging platform for discovering real-world experiences while helping local businesses attract and retain customers through gamified exploration.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature should support one or more of these goals.

- Encourage exploration.
- Increase value for partner businesses.
- Reward curiosity.
- Create memorable experiences.
- Keep interactions simple and intuitive.
- Measure real-world engagement.

Features that do not contribute to these goals should not be included in the MVP.

---

# Current MVP Scope

The initial release validates a simple idea:

Can gamified, QR-powered exploration create meaningful value for both users and local businesses?

The MVP includes:

- Quest discovery
- QR verification
- Quest pages
- Quest completion
- XP progression
- Spendable points
- Adventure Log
- Community Notes
- Business profiles
- Partner analytics
- Reward redemption foundation

Anything not listed above should be considered out of scope unless documented elsewhere.

---

# Core User Journey

## 1. Discover

Users browse nearby and featured quests.

Each quest displays:

- Title
- Difficulty
- XP reward
- Point reward
- Location
- Estimated time

---

## 2. Visit

The user travels to the physical location.

Supported locations include:

- Restaurants
- Coffee shops
- Bars
- Retail stores
- Attractions
- Events
- Museums
- Entertainment venues

---

## 3. Verify

The user scans the venue QR code.

Scanning verifies physical presence before allowing quest completion.

---

## 4. Complete

The quest page presents a clear objective.

Examples include:

- Finding a hidden item
- Solving a clue
- Ordering a featured menu item
- Taking a photo
- Completing a short challenge

---

## 5. Capture

Users document the experience using the Capture the Moment feature.

Supported media:

- Photos
- Videos

Each completion records:

- User
- Quest
- Time
- GPS
- Media
- XP
- Points

---

## 6. Share

Users may share completed quests to social platforms for optional bonus rewards.

Sharing is encouraged but never required.

---

## 7. Leave a Community Note

After completion, users can leave tips, recommendations, observations, or discoveries to help future adventurers.

Community Notes create a collaborative layer of local knowledge tied to each quest.

---

## 8. Progress

Every completed quest contributes to:

- XP
- Level progression
- Points balance
- Adventure Log
- Personal exploration map

Each physical location is represented by a single persistent map pin, which tracks repeat visits over time.

---

# Progression System

## XP

XP represents long-term progression.

XP cannot be spent.

XP determines:

- Level
- Unlocks
- Prestige
- Achievement progress

Example rewards:

- Easy — 50 XP
- Medium — 150 XP
- Hard — 400 XP
- Legendary — 1000+ XP

---

## Points

Points are the platform's spendable reward currency.

Users earn points by completing quests.

Points can be redeemed for:

- Partner rewards
- Discounts
- Merchandise
- Food and drinks
- Event perks
- Limited-time promotions

# Community

SideQuests is designed to create a living map of experiences.

Every completed quest contributes to:

- Personal memories
- Community knowledge
- Local discovery
- Business engagement

Community Notes are central to this experience and replace the earlier Breadcrumb concept.

---

# Partner Value

Partner businesses receive measurable value through:

- Increased foot traffic
- Customer discovery
- Website visits
- Review generation
- Social engagement
- Repeat visitation
- Analytics and reporting

The platform is designed to demonstrate clear return on participation rather than simply listing businesses.

---

# Analytics

The platform measures meaningful engagement while respecting user privacy.

Key metrics include:

- QR scans
- Quest starts
- Quest completions
- Website clicks
- Review link clicks
- Social link clicks
- Media uploads
- Community Notes
- Repeat visits
- Visitor geography (aggregated)
- Reward redemptions

---

# Business Model

SideQuests operates as a B2B2C platform.

Consumers use the application for free.

Partner businesses subscribe to create and manage quests while receiving analytics and customer engagement tools.

Detailed pricing and revenue strategy are documented in **BUSINESS_MODEL.md**.

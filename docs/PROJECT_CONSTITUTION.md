# SideQuests Project Constitution

## Purpose

This document defines the foundational principles of SideQuests.

Every product decision, engineering decision, business decision, and design decision should align with these principles.

If another document conflicts with this Constitution, the Constitution takes precedence until an explicit decision updates it.

---

# Mission

Build the world's best platform for discovering real-world experiences while helping local businesses become destinations through thoughtfully designed, measurable adventures.

We believe exploring your own city should feel as exciting as exploring a great game.

---

# Vision

SideQuests transforms the real world into a place worth exploring.

The platform encourages curiosity, rewards exploration, strengthens local communities, and helps people create memorable experiences they would not have discovered otherwise.

Success is measured not by screen time, but by meaningful real-world experiences.

---

# What SideQuests Is

SideQuests is:

- A real-world exploration platform.
- A discovery engine for local experiences.
- A gamified engagement platform.
- A measurable marketing platform for partner businesses.
- A personal adventure journal.
- A community built around curiosity.

---

# What SideQuests Is Not

SideQuests is not:

- A review platform.
- A coupon marketplace.
- A traditional loyalty program.
- A social media network.
- A generic business directory.
- An open content marketplace.

These distinctions guide product decisions.

---

# Core Product Principles

Every feature should improve at least one of the following:

- Real-world exploration.
- Player enjoyment.
- Partner business value.
- Community knowledge.
- Product simplicity.
- Long-term trust.

Features that do not strengthen one of these pillars should remain in the backlog.

---

# Curation Over Quantity

Quality is more valuable than volume.

The objective is not to have the most quests.

The objective is to have the most memorable quests.

Every quest represents the SideQuests brand.

---

# Community Guide Philosophy

Community Guides are collaborators—not publishers.

Trusted creators, local experts, and community leaders help design quest collections together with the SideQuests team.

Community Guides contribute:

- Local expertise
- Storytelling
- Authenticity
- Community reach

SideQuests contributes:

- Product design
- Game design
- Business relationships
- Quality assurance
- Analytics
- Platform operations

Every published quest remains curated.

---

# Partner Philosophy

We do not sell software.

We help businesses create experiences.

The business relationship should feel like a creative partnership rather than a software subscription.

Businesses are collaborators in building memorable experiences.

---

# Player Philosophy

The player always comes first.

Every interaction should answer:

Why is this interesting?

Why should I visit?

Why will I remember this?

If a feature exists only because it is technically possible, it probably should not exist.

---

# Simplicity Wins

Complexity should be earned.

Before adding any feature, ask:

Does this improve the core quest experience?

If not, postpone it.

The simplest solution that creates a delightful experience is usually the correct one.

---

# MVP Philosophy

The MVP exists to validate one hypothesis:

People enjoy discovering real-world experiences through thoughtfully designed quests, and businesses receive measurable value from participating.

Everything else is secondary.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Engineering exists to enable product quality.

Priorities:

1. Reliability
2. Simplicity
3. Maintainability
4. Performance
5. Scalability

Scale only when real usage justifies additional complexity.

---

# Data Philosophy

Collect data with purpose.

Every metric should answer a meaningful question.

User privacy is a feature.

Business analytics should provide actionable insights rather than vanity metrics.

---

# Design Philosophy

The interface should disappear behind the experience.

The app should guide people into the real world—not encourage them to spend more time looking at their phones.

The greatest success is when the technology becomes invisible.

---

# Growth Philosophy

Grow through trust.

Expansion should happen because:

- Players recommend SideQuests.
- Businesses renew partnerships.
- Community Guides want to collaborate.
- Cities see measurable value.

Growth should never compromise product quality.

---

# Documentation Philosophy

Documentation is a product asset.

Every topic has one authoritative source.

Documentation should explain **why**, not just **what**.

Documentation should evolve alongside the platform.

---

# Decision Framework

Before introducing any significant feature, ask:

1. Does this make exploring the real world more rewarding?
2. Does this create measurable value for partner businesses?
3. Does this strengthen the quality of the platform?
4. Does this make the product simpler or more complicated?
5. Is now the right time to build this?

If the answer to most of these questions is "no," the feature belongs in the backlog rather than the MVP.

---

# The SideQuests Standard

Every quest, partnership, screen, engineering decision, and business decision should leave the platform better than it was before.

If something is not memorable, useful, or delightful, it should be improved before it is expanded.

Quality compounds.

That is the long-term competitive advantage of SideQuests.

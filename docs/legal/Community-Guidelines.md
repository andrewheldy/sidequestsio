## 1. Our Philosophy

SideQuests exists to make exploring your own city feel exciting. Every quest you complete, every
Community Note you leave, and every venue you visit is part of a shared experience with other
explorers. Be curious, be kind, and have fun.

## 2. Respect Businesses and Staff

Partner venues open their doors to SideQuests explorers because it's good for them and fun for
you — that only works if everyone shows up respectfully.

- Be polite and patient with staff, especially during busy hours.
- Follow venue rules (seating, purchase minimums, photo policies, etc.).
- Don't demand special treatment because you're "on a quest."
- If a quest objective doesn't feel right in the moment (venue is closed, too busy, staff seem
  overwhelmed), it's okay to come back another time.

## 3. Respect Communities and Public Spaces

Quests take you into real neighborhoods, not a game world.

- Be mindful of noise, foot traffic, and residents where you're exploring.
- Leave public spaces as you found them — no littering, no property damage.
- Be considerate of other people using the same spaces, whether or not they're playing SideQuests.

## 4. Play Honestly

The XP and Points you earn should reflect quests you actually completed.

- Don't fake check-ins, spoof your GPS location, or reuse someone else's QR scan.
- Don't submit misleading photos or Community Notes to claim credit for something you didn't do.
- Cheating doesn't just break the rules — it undermines the trust that makes the whole community
  worthwhile.

## 5. No Harassment, Hate, or Abuse

We have zero tolerance for:

- Harassment, threats, or intimidation of other users, venue staff, or the public.
- Hateful or discriminatory content targeting race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual
  orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
- Personal attacks in Community Notes or elsewhere on the platform.

## 6. No Illegal Activity or Trespassing

Never let a quest lead you to break the law.

- Don't trespass on private property to complete a quest or get a photo.
- Follow traffic and pedestrian laws — no quest is worth crossing a street unsafely.
- Don't use SideQuests to facilitate any illegal activity.

## 7. No Spam

Community Notes are for genuinely helpful tips, observations, and discoveries — not promotion.

- Don't post unrelated advertising, referral links, or repeated copy-pasted content.
- Don't post content unrelated to the quest it's attached to.

## 8. What Happens If Guidelines Are Broken

Depending on the severity and pattern of the issue, we may remove content, issue a warning,
suspend posting privileges, or terminate an account, as described in our
[Terms of Service](/terms). We try to be fair and proportionate, but the safety of our community
and partners comes first.

## 9. Reporting a Problem

See something that doesn't belong — an unsafe situation, a harassing Community Note, a fake
check-in? Use the in-app report option where available, or email us directly.

## 10. Contact Us

**Email:** [support@miamisidequests.io](mailto:support@miamisidequests.io)
We aim to respond within 5 business days, sooner for safety-related reports.

# Delete Account

**Version:** 1.2.0
**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026

You can delete your SideQuests account and associated personal data at any time. This page
explains exactly how, what gets removed, what may remain, and how long it takes.

## 1. How to Request Deletion

**In the mobile app.** Go to **Settings → Delete account** and tap **Request account deletion**.
The request is made with your signed-in session, so no other identity check is needed. The screen
then shows the date you requested deletion and the date by which it will be done, and you can
cancel it there until it's processed (see [Section 6](#6-changing-your-mind)).

**By email (website users, or if you can't use the app).** The website doesn't have an in-app
deletion option. Email [support@miamisidequests.io](mailto:support@miamisidequests.io) from the
address associated with your account, with the subject line **"Account Deletion Request."** We may
ask you to verify your identity (e.g., confirm your username or reply from your account email)
before processing the request, to prevent someone else from deleting your account.

## 2. What Gets Deleted

Once your request is processed, we permanently delete:

- Your account credentials and profile (display name, username, bio, avatar, social links).
- Your quest history, XP, Points balance, and Adventure Log.
- Photos and videos you uploaded as quest-completion proof.
- Your Community Notes.
- Your privacy and marketing preferences, and your consent records.
- Your location-related records: GPS check-in results, nearby-alert records and settings, each
  device's nearby-alert setting, and quests you saved or hid. (We never store your device's
  coordinates, so there are none to delete.)

Data the mobile app keeps on your device (your location mode, chosen area, and nearby-alert data)
is not on our servers: signing out clears the nearby-alert data, and uninstalling the app removes
all of it.

## 3. What May Remain

Some information may remain after deletion, for the reasons below:

- **Aggregated, anonymized analytics** derived from your usage (e.g., counted in "total scans this
  month" for a venue) — this data no longer identifies you and cannot be traced back to your
  account.
- **Records we're legally required to keep** — for example, if required for fraud investigation,
  tax/accounting purposes, or to comply with a legal obligation, we retain the minimum necessary
  information for the minimum necessary period.
- **Scan and usage-event records** — quest scans and, if you opted into analytics, usage events
  (see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy#4-analytics)) are kept for partner reporting, but your user
  ID is removed from them, so they are no longer linked to your account.
- **Content already shared publicly** that other users may have seen or referenced before
  deletion (for example, a Community Note quoted elsewhere) may not be retroactively removable
  from contexts outside our direct control.

## 4. Retention Periods

Outside the exceptions above, we aim to fully remove your personal data within the timeline in
[Section 5](#5-timeline). Anonymized aggregate analytics already folded into venue reporting are
retained per our standard analytics retention window described in our
[Privacy Policy](/privacy) (up to 24 months), but by that point contain nothing that identifies
you personally.

## 5. Timeline

We complete every deletion **within 30 days** of the request (for email requests, of verifying
your identity).

- **In-app requests** are processed automatically once a day, so deletion usually happens within
  a day of your request. Your account works normally until then.
- **Email requests** are processed by our team within 30 days.
- If you own a partner business account, we review the request by hand first, so we can decide
  with you what happens to the partner's venues and quests. This still completes within 30 days.

You'll receive a confirmation email at your account address once deletion is complete.

## 6. Changing Your Mind

If you request deletion but change your mind before it's processed, you can cancel an in-app
request from **Settings → Delete account** in the mobile app. Because in-app requests are usually
processed within a day, cancel as soon as you can. For an email request, contact us immediately
at [support@miamisidequests.io](mailto:support@miamisidequests.io) and we'll cancel the request if
it hasn't already been completed. Once deletion is complete, your data cannot be recovered — you would need
to create a new account and start fresh.

## 7. Contact Us

We aim to respond within 5 business days.

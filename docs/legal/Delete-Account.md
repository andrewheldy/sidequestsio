# Delete Account

**Version:** 1.0.1
**Effective Date:** July 7, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 7, 2026

You can delete your SideQuests account and associated personal data at any time. This page
explains exactly how, what gets removed, what may remain, and how long it takes.

## 1. How to Request Deletion

Email [support@miamisidequests.io](mailto:support@miamisidequests.io) from the address associated with your
account, with the subject line **"Account Deletion Request."** We may ask you to verify your
identity (e.g., confirm your username or reply from your account email) before processing the
request, to prevent someone else from deleting your account.

> An in-app "Delete my account" option in Settings is planned but not yet available — until it
> ships, email is the way to request deletion.

## 2. What Gets Deleted

Once your request is processed, we permanently delete:

- Your account credentials and profile (display name, username, bio, avatar, social links).
- Your quest history, XP, Points balance, and Adventure Log.
- Photos and videos you uploaded as quest-completion proof.
- Your Community Notes.
- Your privacy and marketing preferences.

## 3. What May Remain

Some information may remain after deletion, for the reasons below:

- **Aggregated, anonymized analytics** derived from your usage (e.g., counted in "total scans this
  month" for a venue) — this data no longer identifies you and cannot be traced back to your
  account.
- **Records we're legally required to keep** — for example, if required for fraud investigation,
  tax/accounting purposes, or to comply with a legal obligation, we retain the minimum necessary
  information for the minimum necessary period.
- **Content already shared publicly** that other users may have seen or referenced before
  deletion (for example, a Community Note quoted elsewhere) may not be retroactively removable
  from contexts outside our direct control.

## 4. Retention Periods

Outside the exceptions above, we aim to fully remove your personal data within the timeline in
[Section 5](#5-timeline). Anonymized aggregate analytics already folded into venue reporting are
retained per our standard analytics retention window described in our
[Privacy Policy](/privacy) (up to 24 months), but by that point contain nothing that identifies
you personally.

## 5. Timeline

We process deletion requests **within 30 days** of verifying your identity. You'll receive a
confirmation email once deletion is complete.

## 6. Changing Your Mind

If you request deletion but change your mind before it's processed, contact us immediately at
[support@miamisidequests.io](mailto:support@miamisidequests.io) and we'll cancel the request if it hasn't
already been completed. Once deletion is complete, your data cannot be recovered — you would need
to create a new account and start fresh.

## 7. Contact Us

**Email:** [support@miamisidequests.io](mailto:support@miamisidequests.io)
We aim to respond within 5 business days.

# SideQuests Partnership Playbook

## Purpose

This document defines the complete lifecycle of a SideQuests business partnership, from first outreach through long-term success.

Every partnership should follow this process to ensure a consistent experience for both SideQuests and partner businesses.

---

# Partnership Philosophy

We are not selling software.

We are helping businesses become destinations.

The platform should create memorable experiences that naturally bring customers through the door while giving businesses measurable insights into customer engagement.

Every conversation should focus on outcomes rather than features.

---

# Ideal Launch Partner

The best launch partners have three characteristics:

### Memorable

The business offers something unique.

Examples:

- Signature menu item
- Beautiful interior
- Hidden feature
- Local history
- Great staff
- Community reputation

### Walkable

The location benefits from nearby foot traffic.

Ideal neighborhoods include:

- Wynwood
- Brickell
- Coconut Grove
- Coral Gables
- Little Havana
- Miami Beach

Clusters of businesses create better player experiences than isolated locations.

### Collaborative

The owner or manager is excited about experimentation.

Founding partners should be willing to provide feedback and iterate alongside the product team.

# Sales Process

## Step 1 — Research

Before contacting a business, gather:

- Website
- Google Reviews
- Social profiles
- Business hours
- Popular products
- Story behind the business

Never send generic outreach.

## Step 2 — Initial Outreach

Goal:

Schedule a short conversation.

Do not attempt to sell every feature.

Instead communicate:

"We help local businesses become part of a city-wide adventure that brings people through the door."

## Step 3 — Discovery Conversation

Understand:

- Business goals
- Customer demographics
- Slow periods
- Best-selling products
- Upcoming events
- Existing marketing efforts

Spend more time listening than presenting.

## Step 4 — Quest Brainstorm

Collaboratively design a quest.

Questions to ask:

What makes this place special?

What should every first-time visitor experience?

What do regular customers always recommend?

The quest should showcase the business's unique personality.

## Step 5 — Present the Experience

Demonstrate:

- Quest page
- QR code
- User journey
- Analytics dashboard

Help the owner visualize a customer completing the experience from start to finish.

## Step 6 — Launch

Collect:

Business information

Images

Links

Hours

Reward details

Quest approval

Generate:

Business profile

Quest page

QR code

Verify everything before launch.

Every business should provide:

- Logo (optional)
- Cover image
- Website
- Google Reviews link
- Social media links
- Address
- Hours
- Reward details
- Contact information

Complete onboarding before publishing the quest.

---

# Founding Partner Program

The first 20–30 businesses receive:

- Free participation for 90 days
- White-glove onboarding
- Direct product support
- Early access to new features
- Founding Partner recognition

In return, request:

- Honest feedback
- Testimonials
- Permission to use results as case studies

The goal is learning, not immediate revenue.

---

# Success Metrics

Review with each partner regularly.

Track:

- QR scans
- Quest starts
- Quest completions
- Website visits
- Review link clicks
- Social clicks
- Reward redemptions
- Repeat visitors

Use these metrics to demonstrate value and identify opportunities for improvement.

---

# Quarterly Review

Discuss:

What worked?

What surprised customers?

What rewards performed best?

Should the quest evolve?

Would a seasonal quest improve engagement?

Treat each partnership as an ongoing collaboration rather than a one-time setup.

# Common Objections

## "People already find us."

Response:

SideQuests isn't replacing discovery platforms—it creates a reason to visit today and transforms a visit into an experience.

## "We're too busy."

Response:

Great. SideQuests can help introduce new customers during slower periods by designing quests around off-peak times or special promotions.

## "I don't have time."

Response:

We handle the setup.

The business simply approves the quest and displays the QR code.

## "How do I know it works?"

Response:

We provide measurable analytics showing customer engagement, visits, and interactions—not just impressions.

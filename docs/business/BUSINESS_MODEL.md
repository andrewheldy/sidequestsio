# SideQuests Business Model

## Purpose

This document defines how SideQuests creates value, generates revenue, acquires customers, and scales as a business.

It serves as the source of truth for pricing, customer segments, go-to-market strategy, and monetization.

---

# Business Overview

SideQuests is a B2B2C platform.

Consumers use SideQuests to discover real-world adventures and complete quests.

Businesses pay to create interactive quests that drive measurable customer engagement.

The platform succeeds when:

- Users discover new places.
- Businesses receive more customers.
- Both sides return regularly.

---

# Mission

Help people explore more of the real world while helping local businesses become destinations instead of just listings.

---

# Value Proposition

## For Users

SideQuests turns discovering a city into an adventure.

Users earn rewards while:

- Exploring neighborhoods
- Finding hidden gems
- Supporting local businesses
- Building a personal Adventure Log

---

## For Businesses

Businesses receive measurable customer engagement.

Benefits include:

- Increased foot traffic
- New customer discovery
- Website traffic
- Review generation
- Social engagement
- Repeat visitation
- Actionable analytics

The product is designed to create measurable business outcomes rather than simply increase impressions.

---

# Revenue Model

Recurring subscriptions provide the primary source of revenue.

Additional revenue comes from onboarding services, premium campaigns, and large-scale activations.

# Launch Pricing

## Founding Partner Program

Limited to the first 20–30 businesses.

Price:

Free for the first 90 days.

Includes:

- One custom quest
- Business profile
- QR code
- Analytics dashboard
- Priority product support
- Founding Partner recognition

Purpose:

Collect case studies, testimonials, and product feedback while validating the business model.

Focus exclusively on high-quality local businesses.

Create unforgettable quests that demonstrate measurable customer value.

---

## Phase 2

Expand within South Florida.

Refine onboarding.

Improve analytics.

Develop repeatable sales processes.

---

## Phase 3

Launch additional cities using proven playbooks.

Recruit local ambassadors and strategic partners to accelerate business acquisition.

---

# Sales Strategy

The sales process is consultative rather than transactional.

Every business conversation should answer three questions:

Why will customers participate?

How will this drive measurable business results?

How easy is it to get started?

The objective is to sell outcomes, not software.

---

# Success Metrics

Business success is measured by:

- Active partner businesses
- Partner retention
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Average revenue per business
- Quest completion rates
- Business satisfaction

User success is measured by:

- Monthly active users
- Quest completions
- Repeat visits
- XP earned
- Community Notes created

Platform success occurs when user engagement and partner value reinforce each other.

---

# Competitive Positioning

SideQuests is not a directory.

It is not a coupon platform.

It is not a loyalty program.

It is not a review site.

Instead, SideQuests combines real-world exploration, gamification, and measurable business engagement into a single platform.

Businesses become destinations rather than listings.

---

# Long-Term Business Vision

The long-term objective is to build the leading platform for real-world exploration.

Local businesses remain the foundation of the ecosystem.

As the network grows, SideQuests expands into larger experiences including cities, tourism organizations, conferences, festivals, universities, attractions, and branded activations.

Growth should always prioritize product quality, partner success, and memorable user experiences over rapid expansion.
